Defense-In-Depth Architecture

100% Security Architecture & Privacy

How Falco Trails SL guarantees enterprise-grade digital safety, cryptographic input protection, and privacy for every international client.

1. Content Security Policy (CSP) & Zero-XSS Guarantee

Our web architecture strictly enforces modern Content Security Policy (CSP) Level 3 directives. Unauthorized third-party scripts, malicious CDN injections, and rogue external iframes are completely disallowed from executing within our domain. All assets and stylesheets are self-hosted or strictly pinned to trusted Google Typography endpoints.

2. Client-Side Cryptographic Sanitization Engine

All traveler inquiries, tailor-made tour submissions, and contact forms pass through an automated sanitization routine in assets/js/security.js prior to execution. This process strips any potentially malicious HTML tags, JavaScript execution schemes (javascript:), and SQL control syntax, eliminating Cross-Site Scripting (XSS) and injection vectors.

3. Invisible Honeypot & Anti-Flood Rate Limiting

To protect our servers and communication channels from automated spam bots and Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) flood attacks:

  • Honeypot Traps: Hidden form fields invisible to human travelers silently flag and trap automated web scrapers.
  • Submission Timing Analysis: Submissions executed under 1.8 seconds (typical of headless bots) are immediately flagged and halted.
  • Anti-Flood Throttling: Clients are restricted to a maximum of 4 inquiries per 5-minute rolling window.

4. Tourist Data Protection & GDPR Privacy Commitment

Falco Trails SL respects the privacy of every traveler:

  • We never sell, lease, or monetize guest traveler contact numbers, email addresses, or travel dates.
  • Inquiry payloads are dispatched directly to our secure WhatsApp concierge and encrypted company email.
  • We operate with zero invasive advertising trackers or covert pixel tracking scripts.
