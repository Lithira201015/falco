Who We Are

Pioneering Meaningful, Private & Responsible Journeys

Headquartered at 50/A, Yagodamulla, Kotugoda, just minutes away from Bandaranaike International Airport and the central transit arteries of the island, Falco Trails SL was established with a singular vision: to unlock Sri Lanka's genuine soul for curious travelers without the tourist cliches.

Inspired by the falcon’s keen eye and spirit of soaring over towering mountains and deep valleys, our team of seasoned trek leaders, certified chauffeur-guides, and bespoke trip designers craft expeditions that touch both heart and soul.
